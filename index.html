One puzzle. Two perspectives.

Drag along a ring and watch the corresponding cube layer turn. Guided lessons connect gestures, notation and cube movement. A glass arrow previews a Hint; a second tap applies the suggested move.

Shape Cues add recognition beyond color. Choose your theme, sound, haptics and device motion settings. Your progress stays on your device.
