Privacy Policy

This policy describes data handling in KoloCubik for iPhone and when you contact support.

Effective date: October 10, 2026.

Who provides the app

The developer and copyright owner is Viktor Shevchenko. For privacy questions, contact shevchenkoviktordoc@gmail.com.

App data stays on your device

KoloCubik works without an account. The current version does not send personal or gameplay data to the developer and does not include advertising, tracking or third-party remote analytics services. Saved games, challenge results, settings, custom colors and event markers are stored locally. Recent gameplay events are held temporarily in app memory and are not sent to the developer.

Device motion and your physical cube

If Device Motion is enabled, motion readings are used on your device to adjust the visual presentation of the 3D cube. These readings are not stored or sent to the developer. My Cube uses manually entered colors; the app does not photograph your cube.

Sharing a result

Sharing starts only when you choose to use the iOS share sheet. You select the receiving app or person. Further processing is governed by the service you choose and its privacy policy.

Contacting support

When you email shevchenkoviktordoc@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you send. They are used to respond and investigate the issue. Email is handled through Gmail, operated by Google, under Google’s privacy policy. Do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains until you change it in the app or delete the app. Device and iCloud backups may retain copies according to your Apple settings. The developer cannot access those backups. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. For access, correction or deletion requests concerning support correspondence, contact the email address above.

Your choices

You can disable Device Motion, sound and haptics in Settings, choose not to share results and choose not to contact support. The game does not require tracking permission. Backup settings are managed through Apple’s controls.

Support pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device information and request times to deliver and secure the website, under its privacy statement. GitHub determines retention of its hosting logs. The developer has not added analytics, advertising, forms, cookies or third-party embeds. All images are hosted with this website. Email links open your mail app.

Policy changes

This page will be updated if data handling changes. Questions can be sent to shevchenkoviktordoc@gmail.com.
